## Pricing consensus (published sources)
- Static vinyl: **$800–$2,400 per 8-hr day** (Blue Line, AdQuick, Billboard Express — identical bands) [High]
- LED: **$1,200–$3,600/day** (same three), up to $4,000 (Can't Miss) [High]
- Hourly: $300–$1,000 (LED Truck Media guide) [Med]
- Weekly: $2,000–$10,000/truck (Billboard Express) [High]
- Event day: $1,950 (MOBIBO Savannah) [High]
- Floor: $50–$300/day shared-slot (Roadrunner, Tampa) [High]
- LED truck acquisition: $50K–$150K (Can't Miss truck sales) [High]

## Structure read
- Highly fragmented; modal operator = 1–4 trucks; largest verified in-region fleet ~10–12 (Mobile Billboard Miami). No operator holds ~15%+ of regional supply.
- Thick broker/aggregator layer resells the same trucks (Blue Line, AdQuick, DOmedia, Billboard Express, Rolling Ads, Rolling Box, Can't Miss, American Mobile Ads, LED Truck Media); ≥4 companies claim "largest fleet in the nation" — recycled network claims over overlapping owner-operator supply.
- Marketplace willingness high: 10+ operators already on DOmedia; AdQuick claims vetted truck operators in every major DMA; the $800–$3,600/day rate card is semi-commoditized across broker sites.
- Demand channels today: programmatic SEO city pages + phone sales; secondary: broker resale, PR wires, chambers/Yelp, franchise recruitment.
